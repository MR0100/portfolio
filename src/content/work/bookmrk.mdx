## Overview

BookMrk is the consumer side of a school-books and stationery brand at [bookmrk.in](https://www.bookmrk.in/) — buy books and supplies by school, class, or publisher, with checkout and live order tracking. Built at Sadguru Soft for the BookMRK brand.

## Problem &amp; context

School-books retail has narrow margins and high logistical complexity. Different schools, classes, and publishers each have their own catalogs — picking the right textbook for a specific school + class shouldn't take more than a few taps. The mobile app then had to carry the order through to a delivery flow that surfaced live tracking back to the customer.

## My approach &amp; key decisions

- **Scalable catalog navigation** — drill-down by school → class → subject is the primary path; search comes second.
- **Responsive UI** with real-time catalog sync and predictable state management across the checkout funnel.
- **Secure checkout flows** layered on the publisher's payment integrations.
- **Live location + maps** to surface live order status to the customer, fed by the [BookMrk Delivery](/work/bookmrk-delivery) driver app.

## Architecture &amp; stack

- **Client:** Flutter (Dart), iOS + Android consumer app.
- **Backend:** REST APIs from the BookMRK platform.
- **Commerce:** IAP integration for in-app purchases.
- **Real-time:** Live location + map updates fed by the companion delivery app.
- **Notifications:** FCM for order status updates.

## Challenges

- **Catalog complexity.** A single textbook can have multiple editions across publishers across school boards — the data model has to support disambiguation without slowing down navigation.
- **Checkout reliability.** Mid-funnel drop-offs are the killer; state management and error surfaces have to be predictable.

## Results &amp; impact
