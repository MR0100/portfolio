## Overview

Lux ECards is the mobile editor behind a multilingual Indian invitation business — weddings, baby showers, engagements, business openings, even obituaries — with templates designed in **Gujarati, Hindi, and English**. Customers pick a template, edit it on a canvas, and export print-ready output without needing a designer. Built at Sadguru Soft for the Lux Ecards brand, published on Google Play by Verdad IT.

## Problem &amp; context

"Canva, but for invitations" sounds simple right up until you try to ship it inside a Flutter app on a mid-range Android. You need a real **layered canvas** — independently movable text, image, and vector layers — exports that look identical to the on-screen preview, multilingual text rendering that handles non-Latin scripts cleanly, and an IAP flow for the premium template catalogue. None of this comes for free.

The Indian-market context made the typography work harder than a default Latin-only build would face: Gujarati and Hindi scripts come with their own baseline rules, ligatures, and shaping concerns.

## My approach &amp; key decisions

- **Custom layered canvas** as the core of the editor. Each element (text, image, vector ornament) is an independently-transformable layer; gestures are routed to the active layer with consistent hit-testing.
- **Preview path = export path.** The canvas-render path and the export path share their geometry math, so what users see is exactly what they share or print.
- **Multilingual text rendering** for Gujarati / Hindi / English. Non-Latin scripts surface a lot of font-baseline and shaping edge cases that Latin-only apps never see.
- **IAP-gated premium templates** layered over a Firebase backend.

## Architecture &amp; stack

- **Client:** Flutter (Dart), Android publication on Play Store.
- **Backend:** Firebase for content + templates; REST APIs for the broader ordering flow.
- **Editor:** Custom layered canvas implementation; PNG/PDF export from the same geometry pipeline as the preview.
- **Commerce:** In-App Purchase for premium template unlocks.
- **Notifications:** FCM for engagement and order updates.
- **Distribution:** Live on Google Play as "Indian Invitation Cards Maker" at `com.luxecard.app`, publisher Verdad IT. Web brand at lux-ecards.com.

## Challenges

- **Non-Latin script rendering.** Getting Gujarati and Hindi text to lay out cleanly across canvas zoom levels — and produce print-clean exports — was its own discipline.
- **Layer system feel.** Independently-transformable layers with overlapping hit areas need careful gesture routing or the app feels frustrating to use.
- **Print-clean exports from a phone-rendered canvas.** Sharing the geometry between preview and export meant the math only had to be right once.

## Results &amp; impact

- Live on Google Play under `com.luxecard.app` (publisher: Verdad IT).
- Brand site at [lux-ecards.com](https://lux-ecards.com/) live, positioning the product as a regional Indian invitation maker.
