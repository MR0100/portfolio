## Overview

A small, focused Flutter project from the early years — an online radio streaming app. Included here for completeness and as a record of where the Flutter journey started; the interesting work in this portfolio is everything that came after.

## Problem &amp; context

Audio streaming on mobile is a great early-career exercise — touches platform audio APIs, foreground services on Android for background playback, lifecycle handling when the user puts the phone down, and a media-controls UX that has to feel right without much surface area.

## My approach &amp; key decisions

- **Standard Flutter audio streaming.** Off-the-shelf was the right call for a small project.
- **Foreground-friendly playback** so audio kept playing when the app was backgrounded.

## Architecture &amp; stack

- **Client:** Flutter (Dart) for Android.
- **Playback:** Audio streaming + standard platform background-playback patterns.

## Challenges

- **Background playback lifecycle** is the classic gotcha here. The platform-specific behaviours differ enough that early Flutter projects always learn this lesson.

## Results &amp; impact

- An early Flutter project; small in scope by design.
- Not in public app stores.
