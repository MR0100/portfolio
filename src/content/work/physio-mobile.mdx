## Overview

Physio Mobile is a German-market telehealth rehab app — targeted exercise videos for people with back, shoulder/neck, and knee/hip pain, plus statistics and reminders to keep users on plan. Built at Sadguru Soft for a German physiotherapist's app practice (published under Robin Brunner).

## Problem &amp; context

Telehealth video apps live or die on two things: the smoothness of the video experience, and the trustworthiness of the data handling. Healthcare data is regulated, users are often older or in pain, and the app has to feel calm rather than clinical.

For Physio Mobile specifically, the audience is patients working through targeted rehabilitation — usually with their physiotherapist's guidance. The mobile app is the home extension of that programme.

## My approach &amp; key decisions

- **Optimised video players tuned for mid-length exercise clips** — fast scrub, clean buffering, no stutter during rep counts.
- **Secure handling of sensitive health profile data** at rest and in transit.
- **Reminders + statistics designed for a non-power-user audience.** Clear, simple, never gamified to the point of pushiness.
- **Targeted programme structure** — body region (back / shoulder / neck / knee / hip), then exercises within each, with progression.

## Architecture &amp; stack

- **Client:** Flutter (Dart), iOS + Android.
- **Backend:** Firebase for user state, content delivery, programme structure.
- **Video:** Optimised video player surfaces tuned for the exercise clip format.
- **Notifications:** FCM for reminder delivery.
- **Distribution:** Live on App Store (`id6448959919`) and Play Store (`com.physiomoboile.training_app`) under the brand's own developer account (Robin Brunner). The Play Store bundle id is `com.physiomoboile.training_app` — the misspelling is original to the bundle and kept as-is.

## Challenges

- **Video player quality + bandwidth.** Smooth scrubbing of mid-length clips on a phone is harder than it looks; tuning the player for the actual content format matters.
- **Calm UX for patients in discomfort.** The product cannot feel like a fitness app or a clinical record — it has to feel patient-friendly and gentle.
- **Regulatory framing.** Health data, even on a non-prescription product, gets handled with care.

## Results &amp; impact

- Live on **both iOS App Store (63.5 MB, 13+ age rating)** and **Google Play (Android v1.0.2, 13.4 MB)** under the **Robin Brunner** publisher account. Last Android build shipped 27 July 2023.
- Store-listing pitch: _"Physio Mobile is particularly suitable for people with back, shoulder/neck and knee/hip pain. With our clear videos, you can perform targeted exercises tailored to your individual training and pain level."_
- Distribution focused on the German-speaking telehealth market.
