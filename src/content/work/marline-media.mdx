## Overview

Marline Media is a Snapchat / Instagram filter and gender-reveal poster business at [marlinemedia.com](https://marlinemedia.com/). The mobile app I built was the consumer-facing creative tool — design a filter or a poster on canvas, order it through the store flow. Built and deployed by me at Sadguru Soft.

## Problem &amp; context

Print-ready exports from a phone-rendered canvas is the kind of thing that looks easy until you actually have to do it. The on-screen preview, the export, and the printer's expectations all have to agree on geometry, colour profile, and bleed. None of that is free, and Flutter doesn't ship it.

Layer this onto a creative tool — independently transformable text, images, vectors — plus an ordering flow with IAP, and the engineering surface gets large quickly.

## My approach &amp; key decisions

- **High-resolution canvas rendering sized to the output specs**, not to the device screen, so exports retain quality.
- **Layer manipulation** for text, images, and graphic ornaments — independently transformable, with shared hit-testing.
- **Native platform APIs for the final encoding step** so print-ready outputs match what the brand's downstream pipeline expects.
- **IAP for unlocking premium filter/poster templates.**

## Architecture &amp; stack

- **Client:** Flutter (Dart) with custom canvas + layer rendering.
- **Backend:** Firebase for templates + user state, REST APIs for the order flow.
- **Commerce:** In-App Purchase for premium template unlocks.
- **Notifications:** FCM for order/engagement updates.
- **Exports:** Native platform APIs for the final encoding step, sized to the brand's print pipeline.

## Challenges

- **Print-clean exports from a mobile canvas.** The preview and the export have to share their geometry math or you spend forever chasing pixel-mismatch bugs at print time.
- **Independent gesture routing on overlapping layers.** Text-on-image with both transformable means the hit-testing logic has to be deliberate.
- **Multi-template IAP catalogue.** Inventory management of premium templates inside a small app is its own minor product.

## Results &amp; impact

- The brand at [marlinemedia.com](https://marlinemedia.com/) is active.
- The mobile app has since been delisted from public stores. Archived evidence (the brand's app-ads.txt referencing AdMob, the website's use of Android-density asset naming) confirms an Android build existed on Play at one point.
