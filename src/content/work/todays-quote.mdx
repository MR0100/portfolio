## Overview

Today's Quote — Be Inspired is the third app in the 1zLabs (Seoul) suite I shipped at Sadguru Soft (alongside Havitglam and Tarot Cat). Daily motivation, smooth transitions, and a leaderboard mini-game embedded inside — the piece that explains the otherwise-unusual "2D-Game" tag on this project's tech stack.

## Problem &amp; context

Quote apps are a saturated category. The differentiator here was two-fold: a) the polish — micro-animations and transitions that feel deliberate rather than templated — and b) an in-app mini-game tied to the quote feed that gives users a "play with quotes / climb the leaderboard" reason to return.

For a Korean consumer audience, content delivery, push reliability and visual register all mattered as much as the underlying mechanic.

## My approach &amp; key decisions

- **Daily content delivery via FCM**, with a local-push backup so users still get the day's quote if the device misses the FCM window.
- **A 2D mini-game inside the same Flutter app** — a leaderboard mechanic tied to quotes, sitting alongside the main feed.
- **Micro-animations and transitions** carried consistently across the surfaces so the app feels like a single deliberate product rather than two stapled together.

## Architecture &amp; stack

- **Client:** Flutter (Dart) for Android.
- **Backend:** Firebase for content + leaderboard state.
- **Notifications:** FCM for daily quotes + local push fallback.
- **Game piece:** Embedded 2D mini-game (Flutter-friendly game engine — Flame is the standard choice in the ecosystem).
- **Distribution:** Live on Google Play at `com.onezlabs.todaysquote` (publisher: onezlabs / 1zLabs).

## Challenges

- **Two products in one app.** A quote feed and a 2D game live very different lives technically; making them feel like one product is its own design problem.
- **Generic name in a saturated category.** "Today's Quote" alone is too generic to find on either store search; the right disambiguation is the `com.onezlabs.todaysquote` bundle on the 1zLabs developer account.

## Results &amp; impact

- Live on **Google Play** at `com.onezlabs.todaysquote` under the **onezlabs / 1zLabs** publisher account.
- Store-listing positioning has three pillars: **Train Your Mind** (daily quotes shaping positive thinking), **Play Games with Quotes** (the leaderboard mini-game — share, compete, climb the board), and **Your Collection of Good Vibes** (favourites view). This confirms the "2D-Game" tag from the legacy portfolio.
- Part of the three-app 1zLabs suite alongside [Havitglam](/work/havitglam) and [Tarot Cat](/work/tarot-cat).
