## Overview

CareShare was a consumer health care and assistance app — pulling activity data from Google Fit and Apple Health, layering on engagement features (camera-based check-ins, push reminders, IAP), and presenting it through a polished consumer mobile UI. Built at Sadguru Soft.

## Problem &amp; context

Multi-platform health-data integration is its own discipline. Google Fit and Apple Health expose subtly different data shapes and permission models, and a consumer health app has to harmonise them while still respecting each platform's privacy expectations.

Beyond the data integrations, the product had a polished consumer surface — camera-driven check-ins, FCM-driven engagement, IAP for premium features.

## My approach &amp; key decisions

- **Cross-platform health-data ingestion** unifying Google Fit (Android) and Apple Health (iOS) into a single internal data model.
- **Camera-based check-ins** as a soft verification mechanism within the engagement loop.
- **FCM for daily engagement, IAP for premium features** — the standard consumer-app commerce + retention stack.

## Architecture &amp; stack

- **Client:** Flutter (Dart), iOS + Android.
- **Health data:** Google Fit (Android) + Apple Health (iOS) via platform integration.
- **Commerce:** IAP for premium features.
- **Notifications:** FCM for daily engagement.
- **Media:** Camera capture for check-ins.

## Challenges

- **Two different health-data APIs.** Same metrics, different shapes, different permission models — unification is non-trivial.
- **Privacy framing.** Health data, even consumer-grade, has expectations about handling.

## Results &amp; impact

- Not currently in public app stores. Listed here as a real piece of client work rather than pretending it doesn't exist.
