## Overview

Foresite is the field tool for **Ukko Agro Inc.**, a Toronto-based smart-agriculture company. The app sits in agronomists' hands as they walk a farm — pulling weather and disease-model data, marking observations, and synchronising back to the Ukko platform. I shipped it as a Flutter developer at Sadguru Soft, working with the Ukko team across continents.

## Problem &amp; context

Agronomy software gets used in places where connectivity isn't a given — the middle of a 200-acre field is not where you want your mobile app to throw a network error. Ukko Agro's customer base sits across North American farmland; the app had to feel as snappy on a tractor-cab signal as on Toronto office Wi-Fi.

Beyond connectivity, the product had to make sense of multi-source data — sensor telemetry, weather feeds, disease-risk modelling — and present it in a form an agronomist could act on while standing in a field.

## My approach &amp; key decisions

- **Offline-first by default.** SQLite is the source of truth on the device. Network operations queue locally and reconcile when connectivity returns — a field worker logging an observation in the morning shouldn't have to wait until they're back at the depot to be sure it's saved.
- **Tight, screen-shaped payloads.** Map and chart screens were tuned to ask the API for what they actually render and nothing more. The difference between a fast field experience and an unusable one is often shaping responses to the UI's needs.
- **WebSockets where it matters, polling where it doesn't.** Real-time channels were reserved for the telemetry that actually had to be live; polling was deemed good enough for slower-moving data.
- **Maps + live location wired to platform APIs** underneath Flutter to keep battery sensible during long outdoor sessions.

## Architecture &amp; stack

- **Client:** Flutter (Dart). Reactive screens; offline-first data layer.
- **Local store:** SQLite, used as the canonical source for outbox-style sync.
- **Real-time:** WebSocket channels for telemetry; FCM for push notifications.
- **Maps:** Platform map SDK integrations for field-area visualisation, accessed through Flutter wrappers.
- **Charts:** Built into the app for sensor + disease-model views.
- **Distribution:** Live on iOS (Apple Canada App Store, `id1604668353`) and Android (Play Store, `com.ukkoag.enterprise`), both published under the Ukko Agro Inc. account.

## Challenges

- **Battery cost on long outdoor sessions.** Field sessions can run for hours; live-location + WebSocket telemetry had to coexist with a phone that still had charge at the end of the day.
- **Sync correctness on flaky networks.** The reconciliation rules between the offline outbox and the server state had to be robust against partial successes and retries.
- **Two product surfaces from Ukko** — `com.ukkoag.enterprise` (this build) and `com.ukkoag.farmer` (the farmer-facing companion). The enterprise build serves both agronomist and grower workflows across the field decision-making cycle.

## Results &amp; impact

- Live on **both iOS App Store (v2.3.0, 188.6 MB, 3.7 ★ on 12 ratings)** and **Google Play (Android)** under the **Ukko Agro Inc.** publisher account. Last iOS build shipped 22 May 2026.
- The published store pitch describes the app as a **Field Adaptive and Integrated Modeling (FAIM)** tool — machine learning that ingests data from a customer's network of on-farm weather stations to drive in-field decisions.
- Part of a working AgTech platform serving North American farm operations.
