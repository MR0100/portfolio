## Overview

KGK HRMS is the internal HR system for the KGK Group — employee directory, leave, attendance, payroll touchpoints — built as a secure enterprise mobile app. I lead the mobile team that ships it as part of my current role at KGK InfoTech LLP.

## Problem &amp; context

An HR app sits on top of the most sensitive corporate data the average employee touches. For a multi-country organisation like KGK, the requirements were strict:

- Role-based access control at every screen, not just at the API.
- Encrypted-at-rest caching for any data the app held locally.
- Clean integration with KGK's existing HR back-office systems.
- Distribution gated to staff — public store visibility for Android (so installs are simple) and internal-only distribution for iOS.

## My approach &amp; key decisions

- **RBAC enforced both server-side and client-side.** No screen renders content the user doesn't have rights for, even before the API responds.
- **Encrypted local storage** with key handling that survives app reinstalls without leaking material.
- **Clean Architecture + BLoC** so the team could move at speed across feature areas without stepping on shared state — the same architecture pattern we use across the KGK mobile portfolio.

## Architecture &amp; stack

- **Client:** Flutter (Dart). Clean Architecture; BLoC for state.
- **Auth:** Role-based access control; secure session handling.
- **Storage:** Encrypted at rest for any cached HR data.
- **Distribution:** Published on **Google Play** at `com.kgkhrms.app` (publisher: KGK Diamonds BVBA). The iOS counterpart ships internally only — no public App Store listing by design.

## Challenges

- **Belt-and-braces RBAC.** The server enforces it, but the client also has to know what to show — and the two cannot drift.
- **Sensitive data on devices that get lost.** Encryption-at-rest with key handling that doesn't get clever and break itself.

## Results &amp; impact

- Live on **Google Play** for KGK staff Android distribution.
- iOS counterpart shipping internally to staff devices.
