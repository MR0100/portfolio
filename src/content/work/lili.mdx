## Overview

Lili is a personal health assistant that connects to a user's wearables — Fitbit and Apple Health — and offers an AI-driven, conversational way to make sense of all that data. Less "fitness leaderboard," more "ask the data a question and get an answer." I led the Flutter build at Sadguru Soft, working with the product's founder.

## Problem &amp; context

Wearables are great at capturing data and terrible at explaining it. By 2021 most users had a phone full of biometric readings they couldn't interpret. The bet behind Lili was that an AI assistant on top of well-modelled health data could do for personal health what a chat interface did for code.

For the mobile client, that meant being more than a dashboard. It had to:

- talk to two very different wearable platforms — Fitbit (cloud OAuth) and Apple Health (on-device, permission-prompted) — and unify them;
- present a fluid, gesture-first chat surface that handled bursts of biometric data without choking the UI;
- handle sensitive personal health information with encryption and permission discipline as defaults.

## My approach &amp; key decisions

- **Two ingestion paths, one internal model.** Fitbit data arrives via OAuth 2.0 from a cloud API. Apple Health data is on-device and only accessible with the user's per-data-type consent. Both feed a single internal health-data model so the conversational layer never has to know where a step count came from.
- **Gesture-driven cards over a chat feed.** The replies surface as a swappable card stack rather than a linear chat log — feels like flicking through a deck.
- **Memory budgeting from day one.** Health data arrives in bursts that wreck naive Flutter apps; the build was tuned to keep widget lifecycles tight, with explicit disposal where heavy data was no longer needed.
- **Encryption + 256-bit OAuth 2.0** as a hard requirement for handling personal health data.

## Architecture &amp; stack

- **Client:** Flutter (Dart), cross-platform iOS + Android.
- **Auth:** OAuth 2.0 to Fitbit's API. Apple Health via the platform's native permission flow over Method Channels.
- **Storage:** Encrypted local storage for cached personal data.
- **Interface:** AI chat-style conversational UI driven by a backing service; swappable gesture-driven cards for replies.
- **Distribution:** Listed on the App Store (UK, IN, ZW and others) under the "AI Assistant Lili" / "Julia Baccarini Santana" developer account. The Android Play Store listing has since been unlisted; an APKPure mirror still serves the build.

## Challenges

- **Reconciling two wearable data models.** Fitbit and Apple Health describe the same metrics differently — units, sampling frequency, source-of-truth rules. The internal model had to be lossless enough that the AI never had to guess.
- **Conversational UI under memory pressure.** Chat surfaces leak. Long sessions with heavy data attached were where a naive implementation would have crashed; widget lifecycles and explicit cleanup kept the experience smooth.
- **Privacy-as-product.** Encryption and explicit permission flow weren't compliance theatre — they were the trust contract users were buying into.
