## Overview

Tarot Cat is a daily-tarot app dressed in a cat — a Korean-market consumer app built for **1zLabs**. The cat talks to you, draws cards, and presents fortunes designed to take a few seconds out of your day. One of three apps I shipped for 1zLabs while at Sadguru Soft.

## Problem &amp; context

Daily-tarot apps live or die on the moment of the draw. If the card animation feels cheap, the whole product feels cheap. If the daily-fortune push doesn't land, the user stops opening the app. Both had to be done well in a small, polished package — and tuned for a Korean consumer audience.

## My approach &amp; key decisions

- **Custom cross-platform animations** for the card draw and reveal — the part of the app users actually remember.
- **Offline-first content database** so a daily reading isn't blocked on network.
- **FCM for the daily-fortune push**, designed for delivery reliability on Korean Android devices.
- **Lightweight Firebase backend** sized to the app's scope — not over-built.

## Architecture &amp; stack

- **Client:** Flutter (Dart), Android publication.
- **Backend:** Firebase (lightweight scope).
- **Notifications:** FCM for daily fortunes.
- **Storage:** Local cache for offline-first daily readings.
- **Distribution:** Live on Google Play at `com.onezlabs.tarot_cat` (publisher: onezlabs / 1zLabs).

## Challenges

- **Animation perceived quality.** The card draw is a fraction-of-a-second moment that decides whether the app feels delightful or generic. Iteration matters.
- **Same-name confusion.** Several "Tarot Cat" apps exist on the App Store from unrelated publishers (Nébula Tarot Cat, etc.) — the right disambiguation is the `com.onezlabs.tarot_cat` bundle on Play and (likely) the "MyTarot 마이타로" iOS app on the same 1zLabs developer account.

## Results &amp; impact

- Live on **Google Play** under the onezlabs / 1zLabs publisher.
- Part of the three-app 1zLabs suite alongside [Havitglam](/work/havitglam) and [Today's Quote](/work/todays-quote).
